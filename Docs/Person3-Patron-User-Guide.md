# Library Patron Guide

## 1. Searching the catalogue (any phone, tablet or computer)

1. Open the library website and choose **Public Library**.
2. Type part of a **title** or a **library code** into the search box and press **Search**.
3. Each result shows its code, a short description and a coloured status:
   - **Green, Available:** you can borrow it now.
   - **Yellow, Borrowed:** someone has it. You can reserve it.
   - **Red, Damaged:** being repaired. You can reserve it.
   - **Black, Destroyed:** no longer in the collection.
4. Choose **View Details** to see the author, artist, age range and other information.

*Tip: no results? Try fewer letters, for example "hob" instead of "The Hobbit".*

## 2. Reserving an item that is not available

1. Open the item's **View Details** page. If it is Borrowed or Damaged you will see **Reserve this item**.
2. Enter your **member number** and the **email address** the library has for you.
3. Choose **Join the queue**. You will see your place in line.
4. When the item comes back, we email you. The item is then held for you ahead of everyone behind you in the queue.

You can cancel a reservation on the kiosk under **My account** or ask at the reception desk.

## 3. Using the self-service kiosk

**Borrow an item**
1. Touch **Borrow an item**.
2. Enter your member number and email, then touch **Continue**.
3. Scan the code on the item, or type it, then touch **Borrow**.
4. The screen shows the due date. Borrow another item, or touch **Finish & sign out**.

**Check your account**
1. Touch **My account** and sign in.
2. See what you have on loan (overdue items are marked red), any unpaid fines and your reservations.

Always touch **Finish & sign out** when you are done. The kiosk also signs you out after a short time with no activity.

## 4. Fines

Late items cost $2.00 per day. Fines are paid at the reception desk.

## Need help?

Ask any member of staff.
